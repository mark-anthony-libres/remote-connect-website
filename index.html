Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused remote-desktop tool that gets out of your way.

Full screen control

See the target's real screen and drive it with your mouse and keyboard, exactly like sitting in front of it.

Direct & low-latency

Connections negotiate a direct peer-to-peer WebRTC path whenever possible, and fall back to relay only when a network requires it.

Two-way clipboard sync

Copy text, images, or files on either side and paste them on the other — no shared drive, no email-to-self required.

Live device presence

Your Recent Connections list shows who's online right now, updated instantly — not on a slow refresh timer.

Nothing to sign up for

Every install gets its own device ID automatically. Share your ID, or type someone else's, and you're connected.

Encrypted by default

Every session runs over WebRTC's standard DTLS/SRTP encryption — control input and video are never sent in the clear.

How it works

Three steps. No configuration.

  1. 1

    Install & open

    Launch RemoteConnect on both computers. Each one is instantly assigned its own device ID — nothing to register.

  2. 2

    Share your ID

    Send your device ID to whoever needs to connect to you, the same way you'd share a phone number.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Type their ID in, they accept the request on their end, and you're viewing and controlling their screen in seconds.

Security

Your session stays between the two of you

RemoteConnect's control channel is negotiated directly between the two devices wherever the network allows it. Video, input, and clipboard data travel over WebRTC's standard encrypted transport, and a session only ever starts once the person on the other end explicitly accepts your request.

  • Explicit accept required before any control session begins
  • Encrypted end-to-end over DTLS/SRTP
  • Direct connection preferred; relay used only as a NAT-traversal fallback
